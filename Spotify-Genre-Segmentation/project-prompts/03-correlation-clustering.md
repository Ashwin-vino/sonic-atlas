# MASTER PROMPT 03 — SONIC ATLAS
## Phase 3: Correlation Analysis, Feature Engineering & Machine Learning Clustering

### Overview
Analyze collinearity among acoustic dimensions, perform dimensionality reduction (PCA / t-SNE / UMAP), and train unsupervised clustering models (K-Means, Gaussian Mixture Models, or DBSCAN) to discover sonic genres.

### Planned Key Tasks
1. Pearson & Spearman correlation heatmaps between audio features.
2. Multicollinearity analysis (e.g., loudness vs energy correlation).
3. Optimal cluster selection via Elbow Method, Silhouette Analysis, and Davies-Bouldin index.
4. K-Means model training and centroid characterization.
5. Cluster-to-genre mapping and acoustic archetype naming.
6. Model artifact persistence in `models/`.
